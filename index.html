How To Post & Track Your Instagram Bio Link

Learn how to place this web link on your Instagram bio or story stickers, capture visitor handles, and track who clicks your links.

1

Setting Up Your IG Bio Link

Add URL tracking parameters when putting this link in your Instagram Edit Profile section:

https://yourname.com/?ref=ig_bio
2

Instagram Story Link Stickers

When sharing a new recipe or workout on your IG Story, use a link sticker with:

https://yourname.com/?ref=ig_story
3

How Handle Capture Works

Because web browsers cannot read private Instagram app logins automatically, handles are captured from the link parameter (e.g. ?ig=handle) or from the name a friend enters when leaving a guestbook note.
